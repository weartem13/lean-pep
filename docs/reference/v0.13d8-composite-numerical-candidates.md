## Scope and numerical convention

D2–D7 already audit a complete exact composite source, covering origin removal,
permutations, oriented/scaled rows, a performance scalar, and omitted zero rows.
D8 adds the NUMERICAL candidate boundary for this same source. It does not add
another source-model normalization and does not call PEPit or a solver.

Input source data are an exact D7 source with its `upper` field removed. The
source contains the existing D5 template, coverage, rows, labels/rules, and
objective. All its rational coefficients, dimensions, horizon, mappings and
identities are frozen. No exact accepted upper is required as input.

The submitted upper contains finite decimal STRINGS for bound, objective
coefficients, multiplier weights and an independently submitted dual matrix.
Assumption and interpolation indices remain integers. Input factors are not
accepted: D1 synthesizes exact factors from each candidate's own target matrix.

Raw means the exact finite base-10 rational represented by each string. It does
NOT mean the exact binary value of a solver's float before serialization.
For example raw "0.1" is exactly 1/10, not Fraction(0.1). Integers and scientific
notation are accepted as strings; JSON numbers, NaN, Infinity, rational strings,
whitespace, and excessive decimal sizes/exponents are rejected. Syntactic size
limits are explicit resource limits, never tolerances or clipping rules.

## Two tracks, neither overwrites the other

The existing decimal parser and reconstruction policy contract are reused.
For each coefficient q, the untrusted producer proposes

  q' = q.limit_denominator(max_denominator).

The proposal is produced independently for each entry, WITHOUT a known bound,
certificate formula, active-set correction, multiplier renormalization, or
identity-enforcing recomputation of the dual matrix. Every changed scalar has
an original string, exact q, exact q', signed delta and absolute delta in the
report. Policy violations remain visible; they do not destroy the raw track.

For each target matrix the unchanged D1 synthesizer returns either exact
weighted rational factors, an original-coordinate negative quadratic witness,
or an asymmetry witness. Failed targets are kept unchanged with an explicitly
non-certifying empty factor carrier so the old checker can reject them.
No matrix averaging, PSD projection, diagonal perturbation, eigenvalue cutoff,
or replacement by a matrix computed from the multipliers is performed.

Factor entries themselves are NOT part of the reconstruction perturbation or
denominator budget. They are exact representations obtained AFTER fixing the
numerical target matrix and are checked by the original checkPSDFactorQ inside
the original upper checker. Their denominators may exceed the policy cap.

## New exact Lean audit of proposed changes

RationalReconstructionAudit.PolicyQ contains a positive denominator cap M and
a nonnegative rational error budget eta. WithinQ is the exact predicate

  |q' - q| <= eta AND q'.den <= M.

The list checker is recursive and length-sensitive, not a truncating zip.
The complete numerical coefficient order is:

  bound;
  objective constant, all value coefficients, ALL row-major Gram coefficients;
  interpolation weights;
  assumption weights;
  ALL row-major dual matrix coefficients.

Both off-diagonal axes are included. Separate exact list comparisons freeze
assumption indices and interpolation point pairs, including count, position,
zero weights and duplicate references. Both certificates have the SAME source
model as their type parameter, so source reconstruction is not an available
operation in this interface.

checkChangesQ_eq_true_iff exposes all those obligations. The audit checks the
submitted ENDPOINTS; it does not formally verify the Python limit_denominator
algorithm, prove that the proposed rational is closest, prove it is uniquely
recoverable, or certify the producer's parsing of the original bytes.

The new adapter's checkRawQ is simply the unchanged D7 checkBoundQ for the raw
candidate. It is independent of reconstruction success or policy compliance.
checkReconstructedQ requires BOTH checkChangesQ and the unchanged full D7
checkBoundQ for the reconstructed candidate. The old source, identity, weights,
PSD, empty-implicit-interpolation, and objective guards all remain required.

Closeness alone never proves certificate feasibility. Nor does accepted
reconstruction imply raw acceptance. Rejection means that a specific candidate
or policy contract failed, not that an optimization inequality is false.

## Concrete regressions

The four committed inputs were explicitly created by truncating prior D7
regression certificates to 12 decimal places. They are NOT live solver output.
Normal regeneration only reads those committed numerical inputs. Only explicit
--create-fixtures imports prior exact data to create the test inputs.

T=1: 86 coefficients, 2 changed by reconstruction. Raw matrix is exactly PSD,
     but the raw certificate identity fails. Reconstruction passes diagnostics.
T=2: 149 coefficients, 44 changed. Raw matrix is again exactly PSD while the
     raw identity fails. Duplicate performance references are retained.
T=3: 226 coefficients, 24 changed. The raw target has an original-coordinate
     rational vector w with w^T S_raw w = -1/83333333325 < 0. Reconstruction
     gives an exact PSD target and accepted exact-certificate diagnostics.
Exact one-step control: 86 coefficients, no change; both candidates pass.

All positive fixtures use M=1000 and eta=1/100000000000. This policy is not
hard-coded in the preparer and is not a Lean acceptance tolerance.

CompositeCandidatesRegression.lean independently checks raw rejection,
reconstructed acceptance, raw negative PSD evidence, and the following guards:
* A zero error budget fails even for a mathematically valid reconstruction.
* A tiny incorrect bound can meet its error/denominator budget and still fail
  the full certificate identity. D8 never converts small residuals to zero.
* Bad factors pass the coefficient-change audit but fail the original checker.
* Inserting a zero-weight term preserves local validity but violates the fixed
  reference-list contract between the two candidates.
* Corrupt coverage is still rejected although the local matrix/identity is valid.

The new Lean checks are proof scripts awaiting local execution, not reported
kernel results from this authoring environment.

## Actual-function consequence

The final wrapper calls the existing D7 actual-function theorem with the
accepted reconstructed candidate. For the original smooth-convex function,
real-valued convex h, global minimizer, physical proximal-gradient run, L>0,
R>=0 and the radius hypothesis, it proves

  F(x_(n+1)) - F(x_star) <= L R^2 * reconstructed.bound.

No eta term is added because the reconstructed candidate itself must be EXACTLY
valid. It does not prove the same bound for the raw certificate. A separate
raw wrapper uses raw.bound only when the raw candidate independently passes.
The three-step imported example gives L R^2/12; this is the old rate through a
new numerical boundary, not a new rate, primal theorem, or tightness discovery.

## CLI and exports

  python3 tools/prepare_proximal_gradient_candidates.py \
    data/fixtures/composite_candidates/pg_three.json \
    --expected-steps 3 --output-dir /tmp/pg-three-candidates \
    --namespace LeanPEP.Imported.PGThreeCandidates \
    --require-reconstructed-pass

Outputs are Candidates.lean (one source, two literal uppers, no automatically
asserted acceptance), raw_exact.json, reconstructed_exact.json, source.json,
and report.json. The exact JSON candidates can each be passed to the unchanged
D7 importer, then D6, D5, D4, D3 and D2. Exported exact candidates carry their
own mathematical check obligations; a standalone D7 acceptance does not assert
D8 reconstruction policy compliance. Keep the D8 report/module when that
provenance contract matters.

Malformed input gives exit 1. Format-valid failed candidates are still emitted
for independent rejection; --require-reconstructed-pass gives exit 2 on a
failed joint diagnostic. Reports always keep lean_checked=false until a user
actually runs Lean. The producer does not authenticate bytes or origin labels.
Exact num/den source normalization may reduce fractions but cannot change values.

## Remaining boundary

No live capture, installation of optional solvers, float-to-original-binary
certification, source coefficient reconstruction, arbitrary raw solver layout,
primal import, constrained rational recovery, interpolation completeness, or
universal reconstruction success guarantee. Some approximate feasible outputs
will produce rejected exact candidates, particularly near singular PSD faces.
That rejection is intentional; no heuristic repairs are hidden in this version.
