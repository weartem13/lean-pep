# v0.14 mathematics and system boundary

## Two proof chains

The current checked dual is transported to a universal upper theorem on one
canonical finite model. The separately checked external primal satisfies that
same finite model and attains the same objective. Their conjunction is finite
tightness. PG1 uses `DataR (Fin 5) 5`, Gram PSD and the complete composite model
assumptions. GD1 uses `DataR (Fin 3) 3`, `RealizesModelQ`, `CoreFeasible` (PSD and
all ordered smooth interpolation inequalities), plus the model assumptions
including radius. PSD plus radius alone is insufficient.

For actual tightness, the current checked dual passes through the existing
actual-function bridge to a general Hilbert-space upper. An independent explicit
witness on ℝ satisfies the entire same function/run contract and attains the
same value. This proves actual tightness without claiming that the witness
realizes every entry of the external solver primal. That latter task,
`actual_solver_primal_realization`, remains `not_run`. A feasible finite Gram
matrix does not automatically realize the specified function class and algorithm.
General interpolation completeness remains an optional future route, not an
additional gate on these already proved witness-based tightness results.

The [index](v0.14-theorem-index.md) gives actual declarations and proof calls.
Captured uppers use current accepted data; historical fixed-rate theorems do not
substitute for their certificate checks. The lower proofs reuse individual
analytic-witness lemmas, not a historical tightness theorem as their upper proof.

## Function and trajectory contracts

The actual upper spaces are arbitrary complete real inner-product spaces.
`IsSmoothConvexAnalytic L f g` records actual gradients, convexity and a
Lipschitz gradient; “Analytic” here does not mean a convergent power series.
Smoothness has type `L : ℝ≥0` with `(L : ℝ)>0`; the radius is real with `R≥0`.
Zero radius is included. The attained examples live on ℝ, not on every fixed
Hilbert space as a separate attainment claim.

PG has real-valued convex `h : E → ℝ`, smooth convex `f`, a composite global
minimizer, squared initial distance at most `R²`, and `IsSmoothProxRun T` at step
`1/L`. The performance is the single final composite difference. The reference
smooth oracle can be nonzero; the h-side reference subgradient is `-g xStar`.
There is no added h-subgradient at `x0`, nor separate stationarity of the two
components. This contract does not include extended-real indicator functions.
The PG1 witness uses `f(z)=Lz`, `h(z)=L((R/2) max(z,0)-z)`, `xStar=0`,
`x0=R`, `x1=R/2`; its reference gradient is `L`, and its gap is `LR²/4`.

GD has a smooth convex real-valued function, global minimizer, exact gradient
steps and the same radius contract. Stationarity is derived from minimality in
the captured bridge. Its one-step witness has `xStar=0`, `x0=R`, `x1=2R/3`,
`g(x0)=LR/3`, `f(x0)=5LR²/18`, `f(x1)=LR²/6`. It uses the existing one-sided
Huber-type construction, including `R=0`.

Historical arbitrary-N GD and positive-horizon PG are completed separate results.
Some historical signatures explicitly assume stationarity or the reference
subgradient rather than global minimality; their precise contracts are retained
in the index. A finite list of live horizons is not the proof for arbitrary N.
The momentum pilot is a fixed four-step final-state claim, not a general optimal
acceleration theorem or a final-query claim.

## Exact source and numerical boundary

Raw finite decimal strings are interpreted as exact rationals using Decimal /
Fraction, without an intermediate binary float. A separately generated candidate
has its own identity. Acceptance of selected data never implies acceptance of
raw data. Direct raw-to-candidate error and denominator policies, including the
original scope of factor-witness denominator checks, remain unchanged; changes
are not accumulated across intermediate candidates. A recomputed structured
residual is labelled as such and never written into the capture's raw residual.

The typed source checks profile, point/value expressions, origin, Gram axes,
ordered references, full row lists and objective. The numerical trace binds raw
data to that source. Normalization and ordering are checked independently of a
hash label. Coordinate transport uses primal `C G Cᵀ` and dual `Jᵀ S J` in their
respective directions; the primal map is not a license to reverse the dual map.
The performance row is `tau - final_gap ≤ 0`: tau is substituted by the final
gap on the upper bridge, and a primal attaining result checks that tau and the
canonical objective agree. Complete rows, zero rows and zero-weight references
are retained. PG selected certificates have empty `interpTerms`; their explicit
source inequalities supply the interpolation content. Exact PSD checks compare
every matrix entry with a nonnegative weighted sum of rank-one factors, not
floating eigenvalues.

PG1 retained upper and primal share the full source and trace. The GD1 exact
upper and new primal come from different recorded solves: full symbolic sources
are equal, numerical traces are not equal. Original GD1 bounds remain
`83333/499997` and `83333/500000`, with positive gap
`249999/249998500000`; the newer exact `1/6` pair does not rewrite them.

Hashes establish byte identity and transport integrity. They alone prove neither
mathematics nor authentic solver execution. Lean kernel checking carries the
proof obligation; the audited standard axioms are `propext`, `Classical.choice`
and `Quot.sound`. `#print axioms` is a dependency check, not a semantic audit of
all hypotheses or of every theorem in existence.

## Interpretation of outcomes

A fixed-family obstruction only rejects that family under its frozen contract.
Raw infeasibility, an over-budget candidate, a resource limit and `not_run` are
distinct outcomes; a timeout is not infeasibility. `known_rate_used=true` denotes
known-rate structured recovery, not discovery of a new optimal constant.
The original default pipeline and D10/D11/D12 negative contracts are unchanged.

No new solve, experiment or benchmark accompanies this documentation. The R14
source byte total is not proof-term size; reused dependency caches and bounded
build concurrency must not be compared as a wholly cold dependency build.
v0.15 research and v0.16/v0.17 stochastic routes remain future scope and have not
been started by this task. No novelty or literature claim is added.
