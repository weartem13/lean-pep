# v0.12B3 universal certificate proof reduction

## Scope

v0.12B2 kernel-checked the closed-form certificate generator on the concrete
instances `N = 0,...,8`.  This slice begins the symbolic all-`N` proof without
replacing the existing exact checker.

The generated certificate has two independent validity obligations:

1. every interpolation and affine-square multiplier is nonnegative;
2. its exact symbolic polynomial equals `bound - f_N` coefficient by
   coefficient.

## Completed in this slice

For every natural `N`, Lean source now proves:

```text
gdChainWeightQ_nonneg
gdStarWeightQ_nonneg
gdSquareWeightQ_nonneg
gdInterpTermsQ_weights_nonneg
gdSquareTermsQ_weights_nonneg
```

Thus all sign obligations of `UpperCertValidQ (gdUpperCertQ N)` are discharged
uniformly.  The remaining obligation is named:

```text
gdCertificateIdentityQ N
```

and means exactly:

```text
certificateRhsPolyQ (gdUpperCertQ N)
  = upperTargetPolyQ (gdBoundQ N) (gdObjective N).
```

The reduction theorem is:

```text
checkUpperCertQ (gdUpperCertQ N) = true
  ↔ gdCertificateIdentityQ N.
```

Consequently, a proof of the identity immediately gives the normalized upper
bound for every feasible finite-GD datum through the unchanged generic checker
soundness theorem.

## Verification boundary

This slice is kernel-verified.  It still does not prove
`gdCertificateIdentityQ N` for variable `N`.  Concrete identities for `N = 0`,
`4`, and `8` are recovered from the kernel-checked v0.12B2 Boolean regressions,
confirming compatibility of the reduction with the executable checker.

v0.12B4 begins the remaining algebra by proving the exact interpolation-flow
recurrences used in the function-value telescope.  The complete coefficient
identity, arbitrary-`N` checker acceptance, and arbitrary-`N` rate remain proof
obligations until their corresponding Lean theorems kernel-check.
