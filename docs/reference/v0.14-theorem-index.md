## Current captured cases

All actual uppers use arbitrary complete real inner-product E, `L : ℝ≥0`,
`L>0`, `R≥0`, squared initial radius ≤R², and `IsSmoothConvexAnalytic L f g`.
PG additionally assumes real-valued convex h, composite global minimality and
`IsSmoothProxRun T` at step 1/L; its objective is F(x_T)−F(xStar).
GD assumes global minimality and each exact step `x_next=x−L⁻¹ • g(x)`;
its objective is f(x_T)−f(xStar). In the table, B is the multiplier of LR².
There are no unproved checker-acceptance assumptions on the current actual uppers.

## Historical general results

`LeanPEP.ActualFunction.IsSmoothConvexAnalytic.parametricGD_upper_radius`
(in ParametricGDScaling) handles every N : ℕ with bound LR²/(4N+2), recursive
`smoothGDIterate`, explicit g(xStar)=0 and radius≤R². Its upper-only signature
allows any real R because only R² is used. The HuberWitness packaged
`analytic_upper_and_oneDimensional_tight` and `oneDimensional_analytic_tight`
add R≥0, L>0 and an actual ℝ witness/IsGreatest statement for every N, including 0.
The upper space is arbitrary complete real Hilbert E.

`LeanPEP.ActualFunction.ProximalGradientScaling.upper_analytic_steps` has
T>0 and bound LR²/(4T), convex real-valued h, the analytic smooth contract,
L>0, R≥0, reference `SubgradientAt h (-g xStar) xStar`, exact proximal run
and radius. ProximalGradientWitnessScaling packages all n : ℕ with T=n+1
as a universal Hilbert upper and ℝ IsGreatest. These are completed arbitrary
horizon results, independent of the five captured cases.

`LeanPEP.ActualFunction.MomentumScaling.certificate_upper_analytic` accepts
an arbitrary rational schedule and horizon N, a checked certificate for
`outputModelQ`, and equality of its objective with `outputObjectiveQ`.
With analytic f, L>0, R≥0, gStar=0 and radius, it bounds the final **state**
by LR² times the certificate bound; physical steps are schedule.step/L.
It is generic soundness, not existence of a certificate for every schedule.
`LeanPEP.ActualFunction.NesterovPilotWitnessScaling.analytic_upper_and_oneDimensional_tight`
proves the specified four-step schedule's final-state rate LR²/21 with the same
smooth/stationary/radius contract and an ℝ IsGreatest witness. Final state and
final query differ at positive radius; this is not an arbitrary-N momentum rate.

R14's aggregate and V12C3/V12D4/V13C4/V13B4 public smokes cover these historical
modules. This documentation only reads their declarations and retained evidence;
it performs no new historical solve. Earlier finite scalar GD, Gram soundness,
Nesterov-chain witnesses and proximal existence/convergence developments remain
available in their original public docs and the 73-smoke inventory; no new
claim about their scope is needed for the captured pairs.
